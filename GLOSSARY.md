# Bilingual Reading

An app for reading books and articles in a foreign language, with the reader's own language one tap away, so practising a language feels like ordinary reading.

## People

**Reader**:
A person with an account who reads Texts in the app.
_Avoid_: User, learner, student

**Native language**:
The language a Reader understands fully, into which every Translation, Gloss and Explanation is written. Either Portuguese (pt-BR) or English.
_Avoid_: Mother tongue, source language, base language

**Reading language**:
The foreign language a Text is written in and the Reader is practising.
_Avoid_: Target language, learning language, foreign language

## What is read

**Text**:
Anything a Reader can open and read: a Book or an Article.
_Avoid_: Content, document, item

**Book**:
A Text imported from an EPUB file, or taken from the Library.
_Avoid_: Ebook, novel

**Article**:
A Text created by pasting a URL or raw text.
_Avoid_: Post, page, clipping

**Library**:
The shared shelf of public-domain Books available to every Reader.
_Avoid_: Catalog, store, collection

**Upload**:
A Book a Reader imported themselves. It is private to that Reader.
_Avoid_: Import (as a noun), personal book

**Chapter**:
An ordered section of a Text, the unit that is translated ahead of time. An Article is a single Chapter.
_Avoid_: Section, part, page

**Paragraph**:
An ordered block of Sentences inside a Chapter, the unit shown in Bilingual view.

**Sentence**:
The smallest unit a Reader can tap to Reveal its Translation.
_Avoid_: Segment, line, chunk

## Help while reading

**Translation**:
The meaning of a Paragraph or Sentence written in the Reader's Native language.
_Avoid_: Parallel text, rendering

**Gloss**:
The meaning of a single word or phrase as it is used in its Sentence.
_Avoid_: Definition, dictionary entry, lookup

**Chunk**:
A group of words read as one unit, not always adjacent: an idiom, phrasal verb, split verb or name.
_Avoid_: Phrase, expression, multi-word unit

**Explanation**:
A short note in the Native language about why a Sentence is phrased as it is (grammar, idiom, false friend).
_Avoid_: Grammar tip, AI explain

**Reveal**:
Showing the Translation of one Sentence on demand, without leaving the reading flow.
_Avoid_: Peek, expand

**Bilingual view**:
The reading layout where each Paragraph's Translation sits directly beneath it. It is the default on desktop and a toggle on mobile.
_Avoid_: Side-by-side, interlinear, parallel mode

**Saved word**:
A word or phrase a Reader chose to keep, with its Gloss and the Sentence it came from. It stays highlighted wherever it appears.
_Avoid_: Flashcard, LingQ, vocabulary item

## Access

**Free book**:
The one Book of up to about 100k words that a Reader may have fully translated without a Subscription.
_Avoid_: Trial, sample

**Subscription**:
A Reader's paid entitlement to translate Uploads and Articles beyond the Free book.
_Avoid_: Plan, premium, membership
