# Claude translates whole Chapters ahead of reading, and results are cached

Translations, Glosses and Explanations come from Claude rather than a machine-translation API (DeepL, Google), because only an LLM can Gloss a word as it is used in its Sentence and explain grammar in the Reader's Native language, and a small model costs less per Book than Google (an early estimate was about $0.10 to $1.50 for a novel against about $12 on Google, before every word was pre-glossed; to be re-estimated). Bilingual view shows every Paragraph's Translation at once, so Translations are produced per Chapter, before the Reader gets there: the opened Chapter and the next one, in the background. Every result is stored and keyed by Text, Chapter and Native language, so nothing is paid for twice.

Translation is done in Paragraph batches with neighbouring Paragraphs as context. Source Sentences are split by rules and Claude maps its Translation onto their ids. Every word is Glossed in the same pass, so taps are instant. A small model does the bulk work and a bigger one writes Explanations.
